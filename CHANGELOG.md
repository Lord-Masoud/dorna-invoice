# Changelog

All notable changes to Dorna Invoice are documented in this file.

## [2.2.0] - Initial Release

### Core Features
- Persian right-to-left invoice management interface.
- Jalali calendar support.
- Automatic invoice numbering.
- Customizable invoice appearance and branding.
- Product and service management.
- Automatic subtotal, discount, and final amount calculation.
- Toman and Rial currency support.

### Customer Management
- Customer information management.
- Customer search and selection.
- Invoice history and management.

### Payment Management
- Multiple payment records per invoice.
- Partial and installment payment tracking.
- Automatic outstanding balance calculation.
- Payment status management.
- Outstanding balance in Persian words.

### Export and Printing
- PDF invoice export.
- High-resolution PNG export (300 DPI).
- A4 invoice layout.
- Direct printing support.

### Data Management
- Local JSON database.
- Offline application functionality.
- Database backup and restore.
- Local customer and invoice data storage.

### Customization
- Custom business information.
- Logo and signature support.
- Custom invoice colors.
- Bank account configuration.

### Distribution
- Windows x64 installer.
- Windows portable application.
